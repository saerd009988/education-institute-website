Why Blue Ridge Tech

What a quarter century of teaching builds

Faculty who stay

61 doctorates on a full-time faculty of 180+, a 16:1 student–faculty ratio, and open office hours every week.

A working career center

Career advising from sophomore year — résumés, mock interviews, co-ops and internships — before 140+ employers recruit on campus.

34 laboratories

From thermodynamics benches to the Center for AI & Data Science opened in 2023, every course has lab, studio, or field hours attached.

A residential campus

Five residence halls for 1,600 students on 120 wooded acres off NC-191, fifteen minutes from downtown Asheville.
