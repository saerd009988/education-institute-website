Why SITM

What a quarter-century of teaching builds

Faculty who stay

61 doctorates on a permanent faculty of 180+, with a 1:18 faculty–student ratio and open office hours every week.

A working placement cell

Dedicated training from the fifth semester — aptitude, group discussion and mock interviews — before 140+ recruiters arrive on campus.

34 laboratories

From thermodynamics benches to the Centre for AI & Data Science opened in 2023, every course has a lab hour attached to it.

A residential campus

Five hostels for 1,600 residents on 38 wooded acres off the Shimla Bypass Road, twenty minutes from Dehradun railway station.
