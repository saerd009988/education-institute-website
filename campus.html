Campus Life

Thirty-eight acres under the Shivaliks

A fully residential campus off the Shimla Bypass Road — academic blocks, five hostels, playing fields and a library that keeps examination hours.

Learning resources

Saraswati Central Library

Collection

42,000 volumes

Core texts in multiple copies for every paper taught, alongside 68 journal subscriptions and a growing digital library accessible across campus.

Reading hall

400 seats, late hours

Open 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. through the semester and until midnight during examinations, with a separate discussion room for group work.

Services

Book bank for scholars

Scholarship holders may borrow a full semester's textbooks at once through the book-bank scheme, returned at semester end.

Residences

Hostels & dining

Five hostels house 1,600 residents. Each has a warden in residence, a common room with indoor games, and a mess run by its own student committee.

Facilities

Around the campus

Laboratories

34 labs & the AI Centre

Departmental laboratories for every course, workshops for mechanical engineering, and the GPU-equipped Centre for AI & Data Science.

Sports

Fields & courts

A cricket ground, football field, six badminton courts, two basketball courts, a gymnasium and a yoga hall. Inter-hostel leagues run through the winter.

Auditorium

Valmiki Auditorium

An 800-seat auditorium hosting convocation, guest lectures and both annual festivals, with a smaller 150-seat seminar hall alongside.

Dining

Cafeteria & night canteen

The main cafeteria seats 300; a night canteen near the hostels serves until 11:30 p.m. on weekdays and later during examinations.

Health

Health centre

A resident medical officer, twice-weekly specialist visits, and a tie-up with a multi-speciality hospital eight kilometres away for referrals.

Transport

City bus service

Institute buses run six routes across Dehradun for day scholars and faculty, timed to the first and last teaching hours.

Student activities

Clubs, festivals and the outdoors

Fourteen clubs run on student committees with faculty advisors, funded through the student activities budget each semester.

Technology

Yantra — the tech fest

Two days each October: a 24-hour hackathon, robotics arena, paper presentations and an industry exhibition. Teams from across Uttarakhand compete.

Culture

Shivotsav — the cultural fest

The spring festival of music, theatre and dance, closing with the alumni night. The battle-of-bands final regularly fills the auditorium past capacity.

Outdoors

Trekking & nature club

Weekend treks into the Shivaliks and an annual Himalayan expedition, led with certified guides. The birding group maintains the campus species register — 94 and counting.

Computing

Recursion — the coding club

Weekly problem-solving sessions, internal contests, and training squads for national programming competitions.

Service

NSS unit

Two hundred volunteers running literacy drives, blood-donation camps and village outreach in the districts around Dehradun.

Expression

Literary & debating society

Parliamentary debates, the annual Shivalik MUN, and Deodar — the student magazine published each spring since 2006.

Walk the campus on a Saturday.

Guided visits run every Saturday at 11 a.m. from the main gate — academic blocks, hostels, library and fields in about ninety minutes.
