Campus Life

One hundred twenty acres in the Blue Ridge

A residential campus off NC-191 — academic halls, five residence halls, playing fields, and a library that keeps finals hours.

Learning resources

Overlook Library

Collection

120,000 volumes

Core texts in multiple copies for every course taught, 68 research databases, and interlibrary loan across the state's academic libraries.

Reading room

400 seats, late hours

Open 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. through the semester and until midnight during finals, with bookable group-study rooms and a quiet floor.

Services

Textbook lending

Scholarship recipients may borrow a full semester's textbooks at once through the lending program, returned at semester's end.

Housing

Residence halls & dining

Five halls named for Blue Ridge peaks house 1,600 students. Each has resident advisors on every floor, a common room, laundry, and campus-wide Wi-Fi.

Facilities

Around the campus

Laboratories

34 labs & the AI Center

Departmental laboratories for every course, machine shops for mechanical engineering, and the GPU-equipped Center for AI & Data Science.

Athletics

Fields, courts & a climbing wall

A soccer field, gymnasium, six tennis courts, a fitness center, and a climbing wall — plus trailhead access straight from campus. Intramural leagues run all year.

Auditorium

Hendricks Auditorium

An 800-seat auditorium hosting commencement, guest lectures, and concerts, with a 150-seat recital hall alongside.

Dining

Commons & Summit Café

The dining commons seats 300; the café serves coffee and late-night food near the residence halls until 11:30 p.m. on weekdays.

Health

Health & counseling center

A staffed health center with weekday clinic hours, licensed counselors, and a partnership with a regional hospital eight miles away.

Transportation

Campus shuttle

Shuttles run to downtown Asheville on evenings and weekends, with airport runs to AVL at the start and end of each semester.

Student life

Clubs, traditions, and the outdoors

Fourteen student organizations run on elected boards with faculty advisors, funded through the student activities budget each semester.

Technology

Summit Week

The spring innovation showcase: a 24-hour hackathon, robotics arena, research posters, and an employer expo. Teams from across the Carolinas compete.

Tradition

Mountain Day & Homecoming

One October morning, classes are canceled without notice and the whole campus hikes the ridge. Homecoming weekend brings alumni back each fall.

Outdoors

Outdoor Club

Weekend trips on the Blue Ridge Parkway trails, gear rentals for students, and an annual spring backpacking expedition with certified guides.

Computing

ACM chapter & robotics team

Weekly problem-solving sessions, internal contests, and travel squads for regional programming and robotics competitions.

Service

Volunteer Center

Two hundred volunteers tutoring in county schools, running river cleanups on the French Broad, and building with Habitat for Humanity.

Expression

Student media & debate

Parliamentary debate, an annual Model UN, and The Overlook — the student newspaper published since 2006.

Walk the campus.

Tours run weekdays at 10 a.m. and 2 p.m., plus Saturday mornings — academic halls, residence halls, library, and fields in about ninety minutes.
