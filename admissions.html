Admissions

Space-available admission for Fall 2026 is open through August 7. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis — most decisions arrive within two weeks of a complete file.

How to apply

The application, step by step

The full process usually takes two to three weeks. Have your transcript ready; everything else can be gathered along the way.

  1. Apply online

    Submit the institute application or the Common App. The application fee is $60; fee waivers are available for students who qualify.

  2. Send your records

    Have your official high-school transcript sent directly to admissions (college transcripts for transfer and graduate applicants). We are test-optional: SAT or ACT scores are considered only if you submit them.

  3. Receive your decision

    Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Most applicants receive a decision within two weeks of the file being complete.

  4. Reserve your place

    Admitted students confirm with a $300 enrollment deposit, credited toward first-semester tuition. File the FAFSA to be considered for grants, loans, and work-study.

  5. Orientation & registration

    Meet your academic advisor and register for classes during orientation. Move-in runs August 21–23; classes begin August 24.

Scholarships & aid

Merit and need, both recognized

Every applicant is automatically considered for merit scholarships — no separate application. Need-based aid requires the FAFSA.

Merit

Presidential Scholarship

Full tuition for the strongest applicants in each entering class; renewable with a 3.5 GPA.

Merit

Dean's Scholarship

$10,000 per year for a 3.7 high-school GPA or top-ten-percent class rank; renewable with a 3.25 GPA.

Need & region

Blue Ridge Promise Grant

Up to $6,000 per year, need-based, for residents of western North Carolina; stacks with state and federal aid.

Activity

Activity Awards

Up to $4,000 per year for club athletics, music, and community service, awarded by the sponsoring program.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is housing guaranteed for first-year students?

Yes. All first-year students who request housing by the deposit deadline are guaranteed a room. Upper-class students apply each spring, and demand is usually met in full.

Do I need SAT or ACT scores?

No. We are test-optional for all undergraduate programs. If you submit scores, they are considered alongside your transcript; if you do not, your application is not penalized.

How does transfer credit work?

Up to 60 credits from regionally accredited colleges transfer for courses completed with a C or better. The registrar issues an official credit evaluation with your admission decision.

What financial aid is available?

Federal grants, loans, and work-study (file the FAFSA), North Carolina state grants for eligible residents, and institutional scholarships. The aid office builds a complete package within one week of your deposit.

Is the enrollment deposit refundable?

The $300 deposit is fully refundable through August 14, 2026 for Fall applicants. After that date it is credited to tuition for enrolled students.

Questions before you apply?

The admissions office answers calls weekdays, 8:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m., and replies to written inquiries within two business days.
