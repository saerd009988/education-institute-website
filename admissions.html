Session 2026–27

Admissions

Round II applications are open for all programmes and close on 8 August 2026. Admission is strictly by merit, through counselling conducted on campus and online.

How to apply

The admission process, step by step

The full process takes between two and four weeks depending on the counselling round. Keep your Class X, Class XII and graduation (for PG programmes) mark sheets ready.

  1. Submit the application form

    Apply online at the admissions portal or in person at the campus admissions office. The application fee is ₹1,200, payable online or by demand draft.

  2. Merit list & entrance scores

    B.Tech and M.Tech seats consider JEE Main / GATE scores where available; MBA requires CAT, MAT or CMAT. All other programmes are ranked on qualifying-examination marks.

  3. Counselling & document verification

    Shortlisted candidates are called in merit order. Bring original mark sheets, transfer certificate, category certificate (if applicable) and four passport photographs.

  4. Seat allotment & fee payment

    Seats are offered at counselling and held for five working days. Admission is confirmed on payment of the first-semester fee.

  5. Enrolment & orientation

    Enrolment numbers are issued by the university. Orientation week begins 1 September 2026; hostel check-in opens two days earlier.

Scholarships

Merit and need, both recognised

Around one in six students at the institute holds a scholarship. Applications for all categories are made after admission, through the Dean of Student Welfare.

Merit

Founders' Scholarship

100% tuition waiver for JEE Main percentile above 95, or the top three rank-holders of each programme every year thereafter.

Merit

Academic Excellence Award

50% tuition waiver for students scoring above 90% in the qualifying examination, renewable each year on a CGPA of 8.5.

Need & domicile

Himalayan States Grant

25% tuition concession for domiciles of Uttarakhand and other Himalayan states, alongside state government post-matric schemes.

Sports

Sports Scholarship

Tuition concessions up to 50% for state and national-level sportspersons, with kit and travel support for institute teams.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is hostel accommodation guaranteed for first-year students?

Yes. All first-year students who opt for the hostel at the time of counselling are guaranteed accommodation. From the second year, rooms are allotted by application, and demand is usually met in full.

Can I apply without a JEE Main score?

Yes. B.Tech seats are filled first by JEE Main merit and then by Class XII (PCM) merit for remaining seats. All non-engineering programmes are ranked on qualifying-examination marks alone, except MBA, which requires a CAT, MAT or CMAT score.

Is education loan assistance available?

The accounts office issues the fee-structure and bonafide letters required by banks within two working days, and nationalised bank representatives are present on campus during counselling week.

What is the refund policy if I withdraw?

Withdrawals before the session begins are refunded in full less ₹1,000 processing charges, in line with AICTE norms. Refunds after commencement follow the AICTE pro-rata schedule.

Are lateral-entry admissions offered?

Yes. Diploma holders may join the B.Tech second year against lateral-entry seats (10% of intake). The process mirrors the regular one, with merit drawn from diploma marks.

Questions before you apply?

The admissions office answers calls on working days, 9 a.m.–5:30 p.m., and replies to written enquiries within two working days.
