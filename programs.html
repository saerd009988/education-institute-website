Academics

Programs of study

Ten degree programs across four schools, each with required laboratory, studio, or field components, taught on a semester credit system with a 16:1 student–faculty ratio.

School of Engineering

Engineering

The founding school of the institute. All majors share a common first year in mathematics, physics, and programming before specialization begins. Every program is ABET-accredited.

B.S. · 4 years

Computer Engineering

Digital design, embedded systems, and computer architecture, closing with a two-semester senior capstone built for an outside sponsor.

  • Credits 126
  • Accreditation ABET
  • Co-op option Yes

B.S. · 4 years

Electrical Engineering

Circuit theory through power systems, RF, and control, with laboratory work on industry-standard test benches every semester.

  • Credits 126
  • Accreditation ABET
  • Co-op option Yes

B.S. · 4 years

Mechanical Engineering

Thermodynamics, machine design, and manufacturing processes, supported by machine shops, a CAD/CAM studio, and regional industry internships.

  • Credits 128
  • Accreditation ABET
  • Co-op option Yes

School of Computing

Computing

Practice-first computing education: every semester pairs theory courses with a build project reviewed by faculty and an outside industry examiner.

B.S. · 4 years

Computer Science

Systems, algorithms, databases, and networks in the core; electives in machine learning, cloud computing, and security from junior year.

  • Credits 120
  • Internship Junior summer

B.S. · 4 years

Information Technology

Networks, cloud infrastructure, and cybersecurity with a required industry internship arranged through the Career Center.

  • Credits 120
  • Internship Required

M.S. · 2 years

Computer Science

A graduate degree with tracks in artificial intelligence and distributed systems, completed by thesis or an industry project.

  • Credits 30
  • Format On campus / evening

School of Business

Business

Case-method teaching guided by an industry advisory board, with required internships and a live consulting project in the final year.

B.S. · 4 years

Business Administration

Foundations of accounting, marketing, organizational behavior, and business law, with concentrations in analytics and entrepreneurship.

  • Credits 120
  • Internship Required

MBA · 2 years

Master of Business Administration

Concentrations in finance, marketing, and business analytics; evening and hybrid formats for working professionals. GMAT optional.

  • Credits 36
  • Format Evening / hybrid

School of Arts & Sciences

Arts & Sciences

Small-cohort science degrees with heavy laboratory hours, taught by the faculty who also anchor the institute's first-year engineering foundation.

B.S. · 4 years

Data Science

Statistics, programming, and machine learning taught in the Center for AI & Data Science, with a capstone on a public or industry dataset.

  • Credits 120
  • Capstone Senior year

B.S. · 4 years

Applied Mathematics

A rigorous degree designed for students headed to graduate study, research, or quantitative careers, with faculty-mentored seminar work from sophomore year.

  • Credits 120
  • Seminar From sophomore year
