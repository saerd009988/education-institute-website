Academics

Programmes

Eleven degree programmes across four schools, each taught with a mandatory laboratory or fieldwork component and assessed under the credit system of Doon Valley Technical University.

School of Engineering

Engineering & Technology

The founding school of the institute. All branches follow a common first year in mathematics, physics and programming before specialisation begins.

B.Tech · 4 years

Computer Science & Engineering

Data structures, operating systems, databases and networks in the core; electives in machine learning, cloud computing and information security from the fifth semester.

  • Intake 180 seats
  • Eligibility 10+2 PCM, 50%

B.Tech · 4 years

Electronics & Communication

Circuit theory through VLSI and embedded systems, with laboratory work on industry-standard test benches and a mandatory final-year hardware project.

  • Intake 120 seats
  • Eligibility 10+2 PCM, 50%

B.Tech · 4 years

Mechanical Engineering

Thermodynamics, machine design and manufacturing processes, supported by workshops, a CAD/CAM centre and summer internships with regional industry.

  • Intake 120 seats
  • Eligibility 10+2 PCM, 50%

M.Tech · 2 years

Computer Science & Engineering

A research-oriented postgraduate degree with specialisation tracks in artificial intelligence and distributed systems, culminating in a full-year dissertation.

  • Intake 36 seats
  • Eligibility B.Tech/B.E., 55%

School of Computer Applications

Computer Applications

Practice-first computing education: every semester pairs theory papers with a build project reviewed by faculty and an external industry examiner.

BCA · 3 years

Bachelor of Computer Applications

Programming fundamentals, web development, databases and software engineering, with a sixth-semester industry internship arranged through the placement cell.

  • Intake 120 seats
  • Eligibility 10+2 any stream with Mathematics, 45%

MCA · 2 years

Master of Computer Applications

Advanced software development with tracks in full-stack engineering and data engineering; final semester spent entirely on an industry or research project.

  • Intake 60 seats
  • Eligibility BCA/B.Sc (CS/IT) or equivalent, 50%

School of Management

Management Studies

Case-method teaching guided by an industry advisory board, with compulsory summer internships and a live consulting project in the final year.

BBA · 3 years

Bachelor of Business Administration

Foundations of accounting, marketing, organisational behaviour and business law, with electives in digital marketing and entrepreneurship.

  • Intake 120 seats
  • Eligibility 10+2 any stream, 45%

MBA · 2 years

Master of Business Administration

Dual specialisation across finance, marketing, human resources and business analytics; admission through national management entrance scores followed by group discussion and interview.

  • Intake 120 seats
  • Eligibility Graduation, 50% + CAT/MAT/CMAT

School of Applied Sciences

Applied Sciences

Small-batch science degrees with heavy laboratory hours, taught by the faculty who also anchor the institute's first-year engineering foundation.

B.Sc (Hons.) · 3 years

Data Science

Statistics, programming and machine learning taught in the Centre for AI & Data Science, with a capstone project on a public or industry dataset.

  • Intake 60 seats
  • Eligibility 10+2 with Mathematics, 50%

B.Sc · 3 years

Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics

A rigorous PCM degree designed for students headed to postgraduate study, research or teaching, with faculty-mentored seminar work from the second year.

  • Intake 60 seats
  • Eligibility 10+2 PCM, 45%
