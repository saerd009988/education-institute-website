About the Institute

A quarter-century in the foothills

Founded in 2001 by the Shivalik Shiksha Samiti, the institute has grown from a single engineering block and 240 students into a residential campus of 3,400.

Our story

Built one department at a time

The institute began in July 2001 with three B.Tech branches taught out of a single academic block on the Shimla Bypass Road. The founding trust, the Shivalik Shiksha Samiti, set one condition that still governs every decision here: no programme would be launched until its laboratories were built and its faculty appointed.

That discipline shaped a slow, deliberate expansion — computer applications in 2004, management in 2007, applied sciences in 2012 — and a campus that added its buildings only as its departments earned them. Today the institute teaches eleven programmes across four schools, holds NAAC 'A' accreditation, and counts more than 11,000 alumni working across India and abroad.

Dehradun gives the campus its character. The Shivalik range rises directly behind the sports fields, winters are cold enough for the annual bonfire night, and the city's institutions — from the Survey of India to the Forest Research Institute — give students a working town to learn from.

Vision

What we are building toward

To be a teaching institution of the first rank in northern India — one where rigorous fundamentals, honest assessment and close mentorship produce graduates who are employable on day one and curious for life.

Mission

How we work

Maintain small teaching batches and current laboratories; revise curricula with industry every two years; keep education affordable through merit and need-based scholarships; and hold every student to the discipline of attendance, practice and review.

Milestones

Twenty-five years, briefly

  1. 2001

    Institute founded

    First academic session begins with 240 students in three B.Tech branches: Computer Science, Electronics and Mechanical.

  2. 2004

    School of Computer Applications

    BCA and MCA programmes launched; the first computing laboratory block is commissioned.

  3. 2007

    School of Management

    BBA and MBA programmes begin, with the institute's first industry advisory board.

  4. 2012

    Applied Sciences & new library

    B.Sc programmes introduced; the Saraswati Central Library opens with a 400-seat reading hall.

  5. 2019

    NAAC 'A' accreditation

    The institute is accredited 'A' by NAAC with a CGPA of 3.18 in its second assessment cycle.

  6. 2023

    Centre for AI & Data Science

    A dedicated centre with GPU laboratories opens; B.Sc (Hons.) Data Science admits its first batch.

  7. 2026

    Silver jubilee year

    The institute marks twenty-five years with an alumni endowment funding fifty full scholarships.
